# CharmerWay research notes

What public research turned up (gathered November 2026). Everything on the site
should trace back to these sources or to the group's own records.
This file is for reference; it doesn't need to be deployed.

## Sources

- **X / Twitter:** https://x.com/charmerway ("Est. 2017", Baltimore MD)
- **School 33 Art Center studios page:** school33.org/index.cfm?page=studios
  - Founded 2017 by Nico and Tyler in a high school televideo class.
  - Charmers: screen-printed shirts and skits.
  - Grew into creative design and direction, talent management, and marketing.
- **CStar Presents page** (old site): charmerway.com/cstarpresents/category/podcast
  - Started spring 2019 and later went on hiatus.
  - Released on YouTube, Spotify and Apple Podcasts.
- **Episode 10, Drew Joiner:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Joc7DW2173g
- **Episode 16, Devin Moreno:** listed on listennotes.com
- **YouTube channel** (from Nico): https://www.youtube.com/c/CharmerWay
